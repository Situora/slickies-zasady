Slickies – privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. The operator is the developer identified in the Slickies listing on Google Play.

Slickies has no ads, analytics or player accounts. Gameplay data is not sent to the developer. Google Play processes one-time Premium and individual animal purchases. The developer does not receive payment details. The Google Play Billing library may send purchase diagnostics to Google using an internet connection.

Purchases

The game locally queries ownership and acknowledges completed purchases through Google Play. It stores unlock status on your device so purchased content works offline. You can restore purchases in Settings using the same Google Play account. A verified refund removes the corresponding unlock. Purchases can be restored after clearing local data; gameplay statistics cannot.

Gameplay and the local network

Solo play works without a connection. When you play with a friend on the same Wi-Fi, the two devices communicate directly. They exchange gameplay inputs, match state, the selected animal and an automatically generated player name. Network addresses are used to find and connect the devices. You do not enter a name, email address or other account details. The developer runs no server for these matches and receives no match records.

Internet, network-state and Wi-Fi permissions enable local multiplayer and discovery. The game uses local UDP/ENet communication; do not treat it as an encrypted service. Use a trusted local network. Vibration permission provides haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

Data on your device

Language, controls, difficulty, sound and vibration preferences, character and arena selections, local unlock state and gameplay statistics are stored in the game's private storage on your device. The game does not send them to the developer, and automatic cloud backup for the app is disabled.

To delete local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Slickies → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. This also removes local preferences and statistics.

Distribution and contact

Google handles distribution and updates through Google Play under its own terms. This policy describes the Slickies game itself. If you choose to send a message or screenshot to the developer, you decide what to share; please do not send sensitive information.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown in the Slickies listing on Google Play.
